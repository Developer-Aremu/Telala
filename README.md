GLOBAL MOTION & DESIGN NOTES

Scroll pacing: the home page should feel like it has a heartbeat — sections alternate between fast (Beats 3-5) and held/paused (Beats 5, 7, 8). Don't let every section animate at the same speed; the variation is what keeps attention.

Color logic: start dark/desaturated (the problem), hard-cut to bright at the Telala reveal (Beat 8), stay bright and "alive" through the rest of the page. The color arc itself tells the story even with sound off.

Typography: one dominant display typeface for the beat-headlines (large, confident, a little industrial — avoid anything that reads as "agri-friendly" rounded fonts). Keep body/subtext small and quiet by comparison — let the big lines do the emotional work.

Sound (optional, muted by default): faint ambient field/wind audio under the hero and problem beats, replaced by a subtle low industrial hum from the reveal (Beat 8) onward — reinforces "operating system," not "farm."

Real footage over stock: every image note above assumes real plantation footage where possible. If real footage isn't ready yet, it's worth delaying launch a little rather than using generic stock-palm imagery — it will visibly undercut the "industrial, real, serious" positioning this whole site is built on.

Performance: all this motion needs to stay lightweight — video backgrounds should be compressed/looping, not full-res autoplay everywhere, or the "alive" feeling becomes a slow, janky page instead.
